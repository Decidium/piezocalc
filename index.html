Unit converterPressure, gauge factors, VW frequency and thermistor

Units are grouped, and the target switches automatically if you pick a unit from a different group. VW digits use digits = Hz² / 1000. The thermistor pair uses the 5k Steinhart-Hart model (A 1.4051e-3, B 2.369e-4, C 1.019e-7).

Overpressure checkBack-solves the reading at a multiple of the rated pressure

The solver works backwards through the sensor's own calibration equation, so it handles linear and polynomial sheets alike. Results are given in both digits and Hz.

GroutingHole volumes and bentonite-cement mix design

1Borehole volume

Displacement covers the galv trimmy, alcathene lines and any cables left in the hole. The alcathene figure is a total volume because each line can stop at a different depth.

2Bentonite-cement grout mix
Mixing order: water first, then cement while mixing, then bentonite slowly last. Mix until lump-free and let it stand about 10 minutes to hydrate before tremie placement from the base of the hole upwards.
